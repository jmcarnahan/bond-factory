# Hard cases for an address-to-roof pipeline (site 1)

Each item: why it is hard, then what the proof of concept should do. "OUT" means declare out of scope.

1. **Flat roofs with parapets of unknown height (4205 of 5405 sq ft).** LiDAR sees the flat deck and the top of the parapet as nearly the same height, so edges are ambiguous. Pitch is 0, so area equals plan area. Do: segment planar returns at near-zero slope, report plan area from the footprint, label pitch 0/12, and give parapet length as footprint perimeter. Do NOT claim parapet height. OUT: parapet height and wall flashing height.
2. **Flat roofs split into several facets with no visible boundary.** This building has four flat facets (151, 2121, 1013, 922) separated only by internal lines (teal) that are small steps or height changes. LiDAR may show a 1 to 3 ft step or nothing. Do: split flats on height steps above 0.5 ft using the DSM, otherwise return one merged flat. Accept that the report's split may not be reproducible.
3. **Internal "eave" lines between flats.** About 65 ft of the 209.67 ft of eaves are internal flat-to-flat edges. A footprint-only pipeline will call them nothing. Do: output eaves for outer edges only and report the internal ones as "height step candidates" when the DSM shows a step. Declare matching Roofr eaves to within 30 percent as the target.
4. **Wall flashing where a flat meets a higher wall.** 344 ft, largest uncertainty (112 ft not visible). Needs wall heights and what the wall is. Do: estimate only from detected height steps plus opening perimeters. Declare OUT unless a step above 1 ft is detected.
5. **Hipped tile section as a separate footprint polygon or merged.** Open-data footprints (Microsoft, OSM, county) often merge the whole building, or split the 35 x 26 hip off. Do: take the footprint union, then segment the hip by roof planes (four planes meeting at a 7.75 ft ridge). Verify by plane count.
6. **Hip pitch is not derivable exactly.** Between 4.5 and 6.5/12 from the report; a single LiDAR plane fit should give pitch to about 0.5/12 if returns are dense. Do: fit planes, snap pitch to the nearest integer rise, and treat the report's pitched areas (948) as the check.
7. **Ridge only 7 ft 9 in long.** Short ridge means the east and west hip facets nearly touch; plane intersection is noisy at LiDAR point spacing of 0.5 to 1 m. Do: build the roof as a hip with ridge length = L - W as a constraint rather than detecting it, and flag if detected ridge is under 3 ft.
8. **Awnings 11 ft deep by 7.5 to 8 ft wide (82 to 87 sq ft).** At 0.5 m LiDAR spacing each awning has about 20 to 30 returns, near the noise floor, and they sit against a taller wall. Footprints will not include them. Do: OUT for automatic detection; optionally detect as small planes below the flat roof level when a DSM exists, tagged low confidence.
9. **Skylights and chimneys (17 dashed shapes, 3 to 4.6 ft).** Smaller than most LiDAR cell sizes, invisible to footprints. Do: OUT. If area matters, subtract a flat 12 sq ft per opening only when the user supplies a count, or use imagery detection later.
10. **Rounding conventions.** Lengths on the diagram are rounded up, areas rounded, totals computed from exact values. Facet labels here sum 5 sq ft over the total, so label sums are not exact. Do: compare to totals only within a tolerance (areas 3 percent, lengths 5 percent) and never score a digit-for-digit match.
11. **Hidden labels.** Some edge lengths are not printed (awning 3 side, steps, 151 sides). Do: treat the Roofr report as partial ground truth for edges and as full ground truth only for totals.
12. **Fields no aerial source can give.** Two-story area, two-layer area, material (Tile, Re stack, Tear off handwritten), and parapet condition. Do: OUT; leave empty or ask the user. Material notes come from the contractor.
13. **Photo-derived plan has 3 to 5 percent distortion.** Our reference plan is a reconstruction. Do: score pipeline output against report totals and labelled edge lengths first, and against plan.geojson only for shape overlap (intersection over union above 0.8 is a good result).
14. **Non-rectangular hip and odd corners.** South eave of the hip slants about 2 ft and has a 1 ft jog, so a rectangle fit is wrong. Do: use the footprint polygon, not a fitted rectangle.
15. **Awning and lobe next to Flat 922.** An unlabelled 11 x 8 ft lobe is folded into Flat 922. A pipeline may report it as a separate facet. Do: merge small facets under 100 sq ft into the neighbour unless pitched differently.
